## Pareto check

### 1. Is any model we ship dominated?

**Yes — in the dictation picker. Nothing new in chat.**

| Dominated (dictation) | By | Every axis |
|---|---|---|
| `gemini-3.7-flash` | `gemini-3.8-flash` | **strict.** Same $0.75/$3.75 and 1M/64k; glossary 76% = 76%; leaks 7/9 vs 9/9; faster at 1.3 s (1286 vs 1822), 8.2 s (1414 vs 1871), 21.3 s (1808 vs 2267) |
| `gemini-3.6-flash` | `gemini-3.8-flash` | same price/context; glossary 76% vs 63%; faster at all three lengths; leaks 7/9 vs 6/9 — **one run, inside noise** (3.6 itself swung 9/9 → 6/9 month to month); plus meta-text leaks |
| `gemini-3.5-flash` | `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` | $0.50 audio/$1.50 vs $1.50/$9.00; leaks 6/9 vs 9/9; glossary 59% vs 47%; latency 968/1075/1410 vs 961/1160/1373 — **the two 3.5 wins are 7 ms and 37 ms, inside overlapping min–max ranges** |

Strictly by the letter, only 3.7 is dominated; 3.6 and 3.5 Flash win one axis each by a margin the
benchmark cannot resolve. I treat those as ties — the same rule `2026-09-08-chat-latency.md` used
to keep 3.7 in chat. **Chat is unaffected:** 3.5/3.6 are already hidden there, and 3.7 vs 3.8 in chat
was measured as a tie on 2026-09-08, so 3.7 stays in chat. Mechanism exists:
`TranscriptionModel.isSelectableForDictation` (already used for 3.1 Pro and dominated Whisper
sizes) plus a `migrateLegacyTranscriptionRawValue`-style forward for the dictation keys only.
Filed as a `model-migration` proposal.

**Not dominated** (checked): 3.1 vs 3.5 Flash-Lite (prices cross on audio-in vs output);
`gpt-4o-transcribe` vs `gpt-transcribe` (4o wins short-clip latency 645 vs 673 and follows prompt
instructions; deprecated 2027-02-26 anyway); `gpt-4o-mini-transcribe` (cheapest OpenAI STT);
`gpt-6-sol` vs `gpt-6.1-sol` (below); Opus 5.5 / Sonnet 5.5 / Haiku 4.5 (a price ladder);
`grok-4.3` vs `grok-4.7` (cross); Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS vs Flash TTS (cross: price vs quality).

**`gpt-6.1-sol` does not dominate `gpt-6-sol`** *(probe, 6 interleaved shuffled rounds, chat
completions, default effort, short research questions)*: 6.1 median **3274 ms** vs 6-sol
**1922 ms**; and 6.1 **rejects `reasoning_effort: "none"`** (HTTP 400, "Supported values are:
'low', 'medium', 'high', and 'xhigh'") — which is exactly what the app sends for `/think minimal`
(`LLMChatProvider.swift:76`). Same price, newer, cheaper cache — but slower and narrower. Both
are frontier points; 6-sol stays.

### 2. Is any model missing that should be there?

- **`gemini-3.5-transcribe`** — yes, the measurement says so: the only silence-safe Gemini option,
  verified serving (benchmark, 0/9 on 9 silence runs, 1/3/8 s). Proposed as an option and as the
  Gemini-only fallback, because the workflow's own ranking puts silence first ("disqualifying,
  however fast or cheap"). Cost of following that rule: glossary 59% → 41%, +550 ms on 1.3 s clips
  (faster at 8.2 s and 21.3 s). Human decision (`other`).
- **`gpt-6.1-sol`** — frontier, *(probe)* serves. **Decision: not added** — owner rule 2026-09-29
  (research price-performance, not coding capability; OpenAI positions it "for complex coding,
  computer use"), measured slower, and it would break `/think minimal`. Reverse if OpenAI
  deprecates `gpt-6-sol`; the migration then must map minimal → `low`.
- **`gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image`** — frontier (half the price, 2.7 s vs 8.1 s *(probe, n=1)*,
  presumably lower quality; 64k context). **Decision: not proposed** — image generation is a niche
  chat mode and there is no quality measurement. Reverse if image turns show up in the usage logs.
- **`openai/gpt-6-sol` as the OpenRouter / custom-endpoint default** — `openai/gpt-4o` costs
  $2.50/$10 on OpenRouter's public catalogue vs $2/$10. The model served via OpenAI *(probe)*, but
  **not via OpenRouter** (no key), so per the audit constraint this stays a finding, not a
  recommendation.
